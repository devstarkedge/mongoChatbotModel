How it resolves related information: during ingestion, MongoDB documents are turned into readable text chunks and stored in Qdrant with their source collection, record ID, and relationship metadata. At answer time, the chatbot searches those chunks, then can follow recorded links to related chunks—for example, from a task to its assigned user. Access rules restrict which indexed records a user can retrieve.
